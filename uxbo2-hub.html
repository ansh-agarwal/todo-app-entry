Getting to Today, three native ways

One approved interaction model, wearing each platform's own clothes. Capture stays home; Today is one tap — or one swipe — away; and Today shows only what you actually chose. Open any build, resize the window, and flip your system theme: all three are responsive and follow light/dark.

The model being demonstrated (already signed off):
  • Capture is the home destination. It opens on an empty field. Nothing asks you to categorise at the moment of capture.
  • Today is one move away — bottom tab on phones, left rail on desktop, plus a horizontal swipe (or ← / → , or 1 / 2 / 3) as a bonus gesture.
  • Today = what you chose. Items only appear there via an explicit “Do today”. Everything else waits in Inbox, which is the one screen allowed to nag.
  • The loop is visible: capture something → “Do today” → a toast/snackbar offers “View Today” → you land in Today with it at the top. That round trip is the whole point of this prototype.

What to try in each

  • Type something into the capture field and press Enter — it appears in “Just captured”.
  • Hit Do today on it, then tap the toast's action — that's the capture → Today trip.
  • Press → / ← (or swipe on a touch screen) to move between the three panes.
  • Narrow the window below 768px to get the phone nav; widen it for the rail.
  • Flip your OS appearance to dark — no reload needed.
