Getting to Today

Capture is the heart — but the second most-used surface is Today: what am I actually doing now. Before designing it, the real question is the transition: how does a user move between capturing and today? Here's how the apps solve it, and a recommendation you can feel below.

01How the apps present “Today”

Pulled from our research list. Two families: planners (you deliberately plan your day) and list apps (Today is a smart filter).

Sunsama planner

A column per day; you run a morning “plan your day” ritual that pulls tasks from a backlog + calendar into Today, each timeboxed. Weekly view toggles to columns for the week.

Steal: the deliberate daily plan ritual + timeboxing; Today is chosen, not dumped.

Ellie Planner planner

Today list beside a calendar; a “brain dump” backlog you drag from. Calm, “focus for today.” Mobile: Today list with a calendar tab.

Steal: Today + calendar side-by-side; drag-from-backlog into today.

Things 3 drill-in

Home is a list of smart views — Today (star), Upcoming, Anytime, Someday — you tap into. Today shows starred/due, with a “This Evening” subsection.

Steal: “This Evening” split; Today = things you starred for today.

TickTick bottom tabs

Bottom tab bar: Tasks · Calendar · Focus · Habit. Inside Tasks, Today/Tomorrow/Next-7-days smart lists. A persistent + to add.

Steal: bottom tabs for the big surfaces; Today as a smart list.

Apple Reminders drill-in

Home = smart-list tiles (Today, Scheduled, All, Flagged) above your lists. Tap Today → the list; “+ New Reminder” at the bottom.

Steal: Today as a first-class smart list; scheduled items flow in automatically.

Todoist bottom tabs

Mobile bottom tabs: Today · Upcoming · Search · Browse, plus a big ＋ FAB. Today is the default landing tab.

Steal: Today can be the default landing; add is always one tap.

Motion / Amie planner

Today = a calendar day merged with tasks; Motion auto-schedules, Amie is a beautiful calendar+todo blend. Powerful, heavier.

Watch: calendar-merged Today is our phase-2 (Calendar view), not v1.

Our capture (locked) us

A calm note surface, first line = title, ＋ New for sections. Today needs to feel like the same app — same type, same circle checkboxes, same accent.

Constraint: Today must inherit the capture identity, not look like a different product.

My recommendation

Bottom tab bar on mobile, left rail on desktop — same surfaces, native placement. This is exactly the “native per platform” call we just made for the checkboxes, applied to navigation. Two tabs to start: Capture and Today — with room to grow into Plan · Calendar as we build them.

Two refinements: (1) Capture stays the home tab — it's the heart, so it opens there; Today is one tap away. (2) Add swipe between them as a bonus accelerator, never the only path.

And the connective tissue you flagged earlier: a captured task doesn't silently appear in Today — it gets there when you schedule it for today (or accept the evening suggestion). Today = things you chose, à la Things/Sunsama, not a dump.
